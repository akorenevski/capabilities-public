# my-notion

Notion over the REST API: pages and databases. Publish markdown, create and upsert pages; create databases, add, query and update rows on the 2026-03-11 data-source model; views, users, comments, attachments and archive. Several workspaces through named connections.

By Alexander Korenevski, under the MIT licence in this repository's `LICENSE`.

Its first version, on 2026-08-10, started from the `notion` capability by Konstantin Žilin in [ai-cluster-one/capabilities](https://github.com/ai-cluster-one/capabilities), under the MIT licence, whose notice is kept whole in [`LICENSE-UPSTREAM`](LICENSE-UPSTREAM). It is an independent capability, not a fork: [`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md) says what survived and why there is no upstream sync.

Run `my-notion help` for the full usage contract and `my-notion doctor` to prove a connection.
